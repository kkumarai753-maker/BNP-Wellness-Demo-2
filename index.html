Still need help? Contact us
How can I request a quotation?

Choose a product and click “Get a quote,” or use the contact form below. The demo creates a pre-addressed email with your selected product and enquiry details.

Can I speak to someone on WhatsApp?

Yes. The WhatsApp buttons open a message to the demo contact number, +91 7058758720. Contact details can be changed before launch.

Are the product photos and specifications final?

No. The current images are illustrative concept visuals and the descriptions are general introductions. Approved product photos, brochures, technical specifications, certifications, and clinical claims must be supplied and reviewed before publication.

Can I download a product brief?

Open a product and select “Download demo brief.” It saves a simple editable HTML product brief. Official manufacturer brochures can replace those demo documents later.
