Urology ICS Standard

FlowComp-NG

Digital Gravimetric Uroflowmeter

Automatic digital gravimetric flow measurement system providing colored, formatted reports with customizable hospital headers. Fully ICS compliant with frictionless operation.

  • High-accuracy load-cell sensor
  • Auto start/stop voiding cycle detection
  • Instant multi-parameter graphical printing
Urology / Diagnostics Multi-Channel

Urocomp 2000 ARM

Urodynamics Measuring System

Complete urodynamic investigation platform powered by ARM processors. Delivers simultaneous cystometry, pressure flow studies, EMG recording, and urethral pressure profiling.

  • High-speed 32-bit ARM processing
  • Automated compliance & leak calculations
  • Wireless or integrated transducer options
Gynaecology Smart AI Optics

Cervaical

Making Cervical Exams Smarter

Handheld smart digital colposcope providing crystal-clear visualization of cervical lesions, automated acetic acid timers, and rapid digital documentation for cervical cancer screening.

  • High-definition medical-grade optical sensor
  • Polarized green filter for vessel inspection
  • Secure cloud archiving and tele-consultation
Oncology Electro-Chemo

Permeos

Targeted Electro-Chemotherapy (ECT)

Fast-emerging oncological tool for targeted management of cutaneous and subcutaneous tumors. Uses reversible electroporation to amplify cellular uptake of chemotherapeutic agents.

  • Up to 1000x localized drug potentiation
  • Minimally invasive outpatient applicability
  • Microsecond calibrated pulse delivery
Neonatology NICU Care

Giggles

Neonatal Phototherapy Eye Protection

Specially engineered soft eye goggles protecting infant retinas from harmful blue-light irradiance during bilirubin phototherapy in neonatal intensive care units.

  • 99.9% blue/UV irradiance filtration
  • Skin-safe hypoallergenic medical foam
  • Ergonomic self-retaining elastic band
Preventive Care Radiation Free

Smart Breast Exam

Tactile Non-Invasive Screening

Quick, painless, completely radiation-free breast tissue assessment device designed for early detection of clinically relevant tissue elasticity changes and palpable lesions.

  • Safe for women of all age brackets
  • Real-time tactile elasticity map generation
  • Zero discomfort, no compression required
